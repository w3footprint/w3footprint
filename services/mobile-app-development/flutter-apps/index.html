Process

From idea to someone building it.

  1. Brief

    You send the idea, designs, code or problem. We agree on scope and price before we start.

  2. Build

    We work in small steps and send short, regular updates on Upwork.

  3. Test

    You get working builds to try, not just promises. We test on real devices.

  4. Launch

    We handle signing, store listing and submission, and hand over the source code.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How much does flutter apps cost?
It depends on scope. We agree on scope and price before we start, and send the price with a clear list of what is included.
Where do we talk during the project?
Everything stays on Upwork: messages, short regular updates, milestones and payment.
How long will it take?
It depends on scope. We give you a timeline together with the price, after reading your brief.
Can you guarantee store approval?
No. Apple and Google decide that. We prepare every submission carefully, fix the problems that come up and help you reach a published app.
Who owns the code?
You do. At the end you get the source code in your own repository, with a short hand-over note.
