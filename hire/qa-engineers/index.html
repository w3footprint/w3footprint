Process

From idea to someone building it.

  1. Brief

    You send the idea, designs, code or problem. We agree on scope and price before we start.

  2. Build

    We work in small steps and send short, regular updates on Upwork.

  3. Test

    You get working builds to try, not just promises. We test on real devices.

  4. Launch

    We handle signing, store listing and submission, and hand over the source code.

Engagement models

Pick the model that fits.

A

Fixed price

A defined scope and a fixed price, agreed before we start. Best for a new app, an MVP or a clear list of fixes.

B

Hourly

Flexible time for work that changes as you learn. Best for ongoing improvements and unclear scope.

C

Monthly team

Steady capacity every month, one or more people on your product. Best for long-running products.

Everything runs on Upwork: messages, updates, milestones and payment.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can I hire qa engineers for a short project?
Yes. Projects can be small and fixed-price, or ongoing. Scope and price are agreed before work begins.
Where do we talk during the project?
Everything stays on Upwork: messages, short regular updates, milestones and payment.
How long will it take?
It depends on scope. We give you a timeline together with the price, after reading your brief.
Do you work with our existing code?
Yes. Tell us the project and the stack and we will say honestly whether it is a fit, and what it would take.
Who owns the code?
You do. At the end you get the source code in your own repository, with a short hand-over note.
