Engagement models

Pick the model that fits.

A

Fixed price

A defined scope and a fixed price, agreed before we start. Best for a new app, an MVP or a clear list of fixes.

B

Hourly

Flexible time for work that changes as you learn. Best for ongoing improvements and unclear scope.

C

Monthly team

Steady capacity every month, one or more people on your product. Best for long-running products.
