KörLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

KörLog is an Android app from W3Footprint ("we", "us") that lets drivers log driving hours, earnings and distance. This page explains what data the app uses and what you can do with it.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a password. The password is handled by Firebase Authentication and we cannot read it.
  • Driving data you enter: session start and end times, break time, platform, earnings, distance and notes.
  • Technical data: crash reports, basic diagnostics, app usage statistics and an app installation identifier. This comes from Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment information.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and see your sessions on your devices.
  • To show your weekly and monthly totals and statistics.
  • To find and fix crashes and to improve the app.

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Where it is stored

Your sessions are stored on your phone and in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes data for us. Google may process data in the EU and in other countries under its standard data protection terms. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows a reminder after long driving sessions. You can turn this off in Settings or in Android.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can change or delete individual sessions in the app. You can delete your whole account and all stored data in the app under Settings > Delete account. This removes your account, your sessions in the cloud and the data on your phone. It cannot be undone.

You can also ask us to delete your data, or to give you a copy of it, by emailing hello@w3footprint.se from the address you registered with. We reply within 30 days. If you live in the EU or EEA you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority (in Sweden: IMY).

Crash and analytics data is not tied to your driving entries and is deleted by Firebase after its standard retention period.

Children

KörLog is not intended for people under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
